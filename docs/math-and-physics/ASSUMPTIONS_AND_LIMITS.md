# Assumptions, interpretation and limits

[Handbook](../INDEX.md) · Source `8058e89e4d35` · Documentation snapshot 2026-11-19.

VoltRL is a research comparison under specified data and information rules. Its code does not establish dispatch feasibility for a real installation, bid acceptance, a calibrated battery lifetime or expected live income. Preserve the policy, objective, source commit and data record whenever quoting a number. [README.md:270–278](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/voltrl/blob/8058e89e4d3516825a400fe86ac45d2e36b6bef0/README.md#L270-L278)

## What is modeled, and what is absent

| Area | Implemented | Practical limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Battery | Six default internal-energy states; full charge/idle/discharge step; fixed efficiencies; SOC bounds | No partial-power action, ramps, outages, AC converter limit, state-dependent efficiency, self-discharge or network constraint |
| Aging | Nominal throughput term, discharge DOD potential, quadratic SOC occupancy cost | No rainflow cycles, empirical lifetime fit, capacity fade or thermal/electrochemical state |
| Prices | Synthetic regime/seasonal process or historical DK1/DK2 hourly series | Exogenous price taker; no price impact, bids, liquidity, fees, imbalance or accepted-volume uncertainty |
| Policy | Estimated finite-state transitions and dynamic programming, threshold, SARX MPC/block baselines | No deep-RL training, exploration interaction or production trading integration |
| End of horizon | Training-median salvage times discharge efficiency | Inventory-adjusted comparison depends on this chosen terminal valuation |
| Uncertainty | Repeated synthetic seeds and specified bootstrap estimands | No generalization certificate for a future market or plant |

## Information and preprocessing caveats

Synthetic decisions see current realized price before choosing the current action. Historical causal policies commit 24 actions from forecast prices before the delivery day. The oracle sees the whole realized future and must stay labeled as an upper bound. The test that changes first-day realized prices confirms unchanged first-day schedules for the SARX policy; it does not prove correspondence to an exchange's auction clock. [tests/test_voltrl_benchmark.py:79–122](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/voltrl/blob/8058e89e4d3516825a400fe86ac45d2e36b6bef0/tests/test_voltrl_benchmark.py#L79-L122)

The historical loader interpolates internal gaps over the complete selected series before splitting it. The committed data-quality table records 12 interpolated hours out of 50,398 per zone (~.02381%). This is a disclosed offline imputation rule; source review did not retrieve the raw OPSD input or independently identify the causes of those gaps. Complete-day trimming subsequently removes 46 boundary hours; both zones have 15,120 historical test hours. [voltrl_benchmark.py:1083–1118](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/voltrl/blob/8058e89e4d3516825a400fe86ac45d2e36b6bef0/voltrl_benchmark.py#L1083-L1118) [results_revision2/data_quality.csv:1–3](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/voltrl/blob/8058e89e4d3516825a400fe86ac45d2e36b6bef0/results_revision2/data_quality.csv#L1-L3) [results_revision2/case_diagnostics.csv:32–33](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/voltrl/blob/8058e89e4d3516825a400fe86ac45d2e36b6bef0/results_revision2/case_diagnostics.csv#L32-L33)

The timestamp named `information_available_through_delivery_timestamp` is the previous delivery hour in code. No publication-latency or market-gate mapping exists. A future operational study must define those times explicitly, use only information demonstrably available then and re-evaluate forecast schedules. [voltrl_benchmark.py:712–811](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/voltrl/blob/8058e89e4d3516825a400fe86ac45d2e36b6bef0/voltrl_benchmark.py#L712-L811)

## Stored result interpretation

The revision-2 synthetic table records SARX MPC mean annualized adjusted profit of 5,133,511.75 and hour-aware MDP 4,620,099.80 in the synthetic currency scale. The paired hour-aware-minus-SARX mean is −513,411.95 across 30 seeds. These are archived benchmark results generated by the manifest's older source snapshot; the documentation audit did not regenerate all seeds. [results_revision2/synthetic_summary.csv:1–7](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/voltrl/blob/8058e89e4d3516825a400fe86ac45d2e36b6bef0/results_revision2/synthetic_summary.csv#L1-L7) [results_revision2/paired_seed_comparisons.csv:1–5](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/voltrl/blob/8058e89e4d3516825a400fe86ac45d2e36b6bef0/results_revision2/paired_seed_comparisons.csv#L1-L5)

The historical table is a different experiment: SARX block annualized adjusted profit is 129,293.75 EUR in DK1 and 187,344.12 EUR in DK2. Seasonal-mean leads the listed causal DK1 policies (407,482.68 EUR), while persistence leads DK2 (420,479.10 EUR). Better price MAE therefore does not by itself imply better dispatch profit. The table's oracle rows share the protocol label column but still use full future realized prices and are not causal competitors. ## Audit limits that must stay visible

- The artifact audit verifies files declared in `outputs` and their inventory records. It does not inventory every extra file that happens to be in a result directory. CSV line endings are canonicalized CRLF→LF; figures remain byte-exact.
- Clean software provenance covers the three declared default files at the recorded Git commit. It does not automatically attest all helpers, all working-tree changes or the complete environment.
- The experiment contract checks complete candidate/fold coverage and consistency of selected K. It does not rerun NLL fitting or prove the selected K is the true minimum or an interior optimum.
- The synthetic statistics contract recomputes summaries and checks interval ordering/mean inclusion. It does not reproduce bootstrap percentile endpoints or recompute raw battery trajectories.
- The historical contract reconstructs error arithmetic and timing consistency in recorded rows. It does not establish that forecasts were generated from the claimed information set or verify an auction publication calendar by itself.

There is no spatial PDE or physical boundary-value solver to validate. Numerical agreement, causal dependency tests, source integrity and measured validation answer different questions. Keep each result attached to the check that actually supports it.
