## Operational failure handling

A source-hash mismatch, required-policy unknown, unsigned candidate under a signature rule, failed required check, stale candidate head or unproven deployment is a reason to preserve the reviewable work and resolve that specific gate. It is not a reason to lower the standard or relabel a historical successful run. The [release playbook](CI_CD_AND_RELEASE_PLAYBOOK.md) specifies the required observations.

Output directories can be overwritten by experimental commands. Write new runs outside committed result folders, retain their configuration/provenance, and compare meaning before comparing numbers. For verifier failures, restore known intended bytes or create a new declared experiment; do not use `--write` to erase evidence of drift. [Troubleshooting](../developer/TROUBLESHOOTING.md).

## Scope and severity

VR-01/02 are high-priority scientific interpretation boundaries, not claims of a demonstrated live-system failure. VR-03 is a concrete labeling defect for nondefault runs. VR-04/05/06/07 are audit, maintenance and recovery gaps. VR-08 is documentation evolution; VR-09 is exploratory research. The [roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md) assigns proposed horizons, roles, dependencies, effort and measurable checks without implementing these proposals here.
